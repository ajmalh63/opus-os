## 11. Risks & Red Flags — Before You Sign First MBBS File

- **Non-WDOMS or 4-year MBBS** — automatic NMC invalid. Check duration + internship in offer letter.
- **“No NEET” or “NEET after admission”** — NMC requires NEET *before* admission for eligibility.
- **“Guaranteed FMGE pass”** — FMGE pass ~25-30% overall; never guarantee.
- **“Donation/capitation”** — NMC MBBS abroad has no donation; fee is to university directly.
- **Sub-agent chain** — MOKSH/Rus are direct, but verify no hidden sub-agent is taking cut from student.
- **Visa renewal cost** — Georgia/Russia yearly TRC/visa ~$300-500 — disclose, don’t hide.
- **Currency risk** — USD/RUB/GEL fluctuation — fee in USD, student pays in INR at that day’s rate.

**Due diligence per file:** WDOMS screenshot + NEET scorecard + PCB % calc + offer letter with 54-mo + internship line + visa copy → all in `Journey Vault`.

## 18. When Is the Right Time to Start Preparing for MBBS Abroad? (Nizamabad — Telangana Families)

### The Golden Rule: NEET Before Admission

NMC requires **NEET qualified *before* the admission letter date** for your degree to be valid for FMGE/NExT in India.

- **NEET exam:** 1st Sunday of **May** every year
- **NEET result:** June (2nd-3rd week)
- **MBBS abroad admission window:** June–September for **September–October intake**

If you start preparing *after* NEET, you lose 6 months and often the September intake. **Start while in Class 11.**

### Ideal Start by Current Class

| If you are in... | Target Intake | Start Now? | What to do now (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 11 (2025–26)** | **Sept 2027** (after 12th + NEET May 2027) | **Now** — best time | NEET foundation + PCB 50% focus + passport apply (45 days) + shortlist 3 WDOMS-verified colleges (Georgia, Russia, Kazakhstan) in `StudentProfileWizard` (60%+). |
| **Class 12 (2025–26) appearing + NEET May 2026** | **Sept 2026** (closing *now*) or **Feb/Mar 2027** backup | **Immediately** | WDOMS check + documents + visa. If NEET 2026 qualified, you can still grab **Sept 2026 last seats (closes Aug 20–31)** or lock **Feb 2027** (Georgia/Kazakhstan). |
| **12th passed + NEET qualified (2025) with gap** | **Sept 2026 or Feb 2027** | **Now** | Gap explanation (`gapYears/gapReason` in portal) + NEET scorecard + 54-month compliance docs. |
| **12th passed, NEET not qualified** | **NEET May 2027 → Sept 2027** | **Now** | Full NEET prep year — **do not take admission before NEET** — it will be NMC-invalid. |

**Advice for your catchment:** Start in **Class 11 summer**. By 12th mid-term, your `StudentProfileWizard` should already be 60%+ (`NEET target, PCB %, passport, budget`, `targetCountry`, `tuitionBudget`) — then September application is one click, not a scramble.

### Month-by-Month: What “Start Early” Actually Means

**If targeting Sept 2027 (Class 11 now):**
- **Aug–Oct 2026 (Now):** NEET foundation, `StudentProfileWizard` 60% (PCB %, targetCountry, budget), passport apply (45 days), shortlist 3 WDOMS-verified colleges → screenshot to `Journey Vault`.
- **Nov–Feb 2027:** NEET mocks, 12th pre-boards, sponsor bank docs ready, SOP draft for gap if any.
- **Mar–Apr 2027:** 12th Boards, final NEET sprint.
- **May 2027:** NEET exam → **June:** NEET result → **June–July:** Offer letters (we do 3 parallel: Georgia + Russia + Kazakhstan) → **July–Aug:** Attestation (State HRD → MEA) + visa → **Sept:** Fly.

**If targeting Sept 2026 (late, but possible):**
- You have **~10–15 days** for last seats in Georgia/Kazakhstan Sept 2026 — need NEET 2025/2026 qualified + passport + PCB 50% *today*. Otherwise, lock **Feb 2027** now and use Sept–Jan to close documents without panic.

### Deadlines You Cannot Miss

- **NEET qualification:** Must have **before** admission letter date — backdated NEET after admission = NMC invalid.
- **Visa:** Georgia 25 days, Russia 30–40 days — don’t apply in last 2 weeks of August for September.
- **NMC Gazette:** Offer letter must show **54 months + 12-month internship** — if it says “5 years” without internship, ask for corrected letter before paying.
